WORK / DIGITAL EXPERIENCES / GOODLOOKS AUTO SPA

GoodLooks Auto Spa

Automotive website design

GoodLooks Auto Spa is a family-run automotive detailing and protection shop in Watertown, CT. I independently designed the website concept in Figma, developing the visual direction, page layouts, and responsive designs from the ground up. The design balances a modern, user-centered experience with the quality of the brand.

The concept includes dedicated service pages, a VIP maintenance program, a vehicle portfolio, and dynamic service-area pages, with a focus on intuitive navigation, visual consistency, and scalability across desktop and mobile.
