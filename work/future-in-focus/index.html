Future in Focus

FILM FESTIVAL BRANDING

BRAND IDENTITY · ART DIRECTION · DIGITAL DESIGN

Future in Focus is a film festival centered on the impact of climate change on animals and the natural world. The project explores how a visual identity can communicate a serious subject while remaining engaging, approachable, and cohesive.

The identity is built around expressive illustrations of endangered species, paired with bold typography and graphic elements to create a distinct visual language. This system extends across posters, name tags, business stationery, food packaging, event programs, and digital experiences.

A fully prototyped app and website, designed in Figma, extend the identity into a cohesive digital experience.
