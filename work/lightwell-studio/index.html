Lightwell Studio

INTERIOR DESIGN BRAND IDENTITY

BRAND IDENTITY · ART DIRECTION · PRINT & DIGITAL DESIGN

Lightwell Studio is a boutique interior design studio focused on creating thoughtful, refined spaces shaped by natural light, materials, and intentional details. The goal was to develop an identity that feels warm and sophisticated while reflecting the studio’s architectural approach to design.

The visual identity centers around an organic window-inspired mark, paired with a refined typographic system and a muted palette of warm ivory, stone, olive, walnut, and charcoal. Hand-drawn elements and subtle textures add a tactile quality to the otherwise structured system.

The identity was extended across signage, stationery, patterned materials, business cards, and digital applications to create a cohesive brand experience across both physical and digital touchpoints.
