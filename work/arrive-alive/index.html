Arrive Alive

PSA CAMPAIGN · “THINK BEFORE YOU DRINK: ARRIVE ALIVE”

CAMPAIGN DESIGN · ART DIRECTION · MOTION DESIGN

Arrive Alive is a public service campaign addressing drunk driving through targeted research and visual communication. I researched the social issue and its impact on my target audience of men ages 20–30 to inform the campaign’s creative direction and messaging.

I developed the PSA entirely in Adobe Illustrator and After Effects, creating original illustrations, campaign visuals, and animated typography to communicate the message through motion.

I brought the Arrive Alive identity to life in Adobe After Effects through animated typography, exploring movement, pacing, and transitions to create a dynamic visual expression of the campaign's message.

PSA VIDEO

I illustrated each scene in Adobe Illustrator before animating the artwork, transitions, and compositions in After Effects. Bringing the PSA to life across just two platforms challenged me to push my technical skills and use motion to communicate an urgent message effectively.
