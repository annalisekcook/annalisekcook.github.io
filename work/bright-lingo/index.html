Bright Lingo

UI/UX language learning app

Bright Lingo is a French language-learning app designed to make building new language skills feel intuitive, engaging and approachable. The project covers the full product design process, from research and brand strategy to concept sketches, a complete UI system and an interactive prototype.

Design process

01 · The challenge

Most language-learning apps offer little customization and few ways to learn. This project asked: how can a clear user flow and progress tracking make learning a new language more effective?

02 · Research & strategy

I defined the brand's vision, audience and value proposition. The app is designed for adults learning a new language, with AI-generated lessons that adapt to each user.

03 · Brand personality

Three archetypes shaped the brand: the Sage (growth through learning), the Hero (reaching goals faster) and the Explorer (learning at your own pace).

04 · UI system

I built a full UI kit of input, output and helper elements, along with a custom icon set for navigation, lessons and achievements.
