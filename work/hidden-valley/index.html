WORK / BRAND IDENTITY / HIDDEN VALLEY

Hidden Valley

Restaurant brand refresh

Working within Hidden Valley Mini Golf’s established brand identity, I developed a cohesive visual system across menus, print materials, and digital marketing. From the main menu and beer menu to seasonal specials, ice cream menus, and social media campaigns, each piece balanced existing brand elements with refined layouts, typography, and consistent visual execution.

The project extended across signage, brochures, and website development, creating a more unified customer experience across print and digital touchpoints.
