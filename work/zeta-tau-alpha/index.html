Zeta Tau Alpha

Brand & Communications Director

As Vice President of Communications for Zeta Tau Alpha, I led the chapter’s visual and digital presence, developing a cohesive brand identity across social platforms and chapter communications. I designed promotional graphics, recruitment materials, event campaigns, and branded content while photographing, editing, and producing video content using Adobe Creative Suite.

I also managed brand collaborations and content strategy, maintaining a consistent visual direction that strengthened the chapter’s online presence.
