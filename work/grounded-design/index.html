Grounded Design

CLIENT BRAND IDENTITY

BRAND IDENTITY · ART DIRECTION · DIGITAL DESIGN

Grounded Design is a branding project built around the client’s appreciation for nature, organic forms, and sculptural rock formations. The goal was to create a warm, intentional identity that balances approachability with structure and personality.

Beginning with sketching, typography, color exploration, and an earthy mood board, I developed an organic logo mark and two distinct creative directions, each offering a unique interpretation of the brand.

The final identity extends across website concepts, stationery, signage, and supporting brand assets, demonstrating its versatility across digital and physical applications.
