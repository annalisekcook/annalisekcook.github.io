Paper Treasures

HANDMADE STATIONERY BRAND

BRAND IDENTITY · WEB DESIGN · PACKAGING

Paper Treasures is a handmade stationery brand built around thoughtful, personal design. Hand-drawn botanical illustrations, a soft color palette and refined typography turn everyday stationery into keepsakes.

The identity extends across greeting cards, packaging and a fully designed website, creating one cohesive experience in print and online, and celebrating the value of slowing down in an increasingly digital world.

INTERACTIVE WEBSITE

An e-commerce site for browsing collections and designing custom cards, built around the tagline "Where words become gifts." Scroll and click through the Figma prototype to explore the experience. Some features are simplified for demonstration.
