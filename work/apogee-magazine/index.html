WORK / VISUAL COMMUNICATION / APOGEE MAGAZINE

Apogee Magazine

Lead Designer · 2025 edition

Nominated by my professors to serve as Lead Designer for the 2025 edition of Apogee Magazine, I led the publication’s visual direction, developing its typographic system, layouts, and visual hierarchy to bring together student poetry and artwork.

The design explores typography as a key visual element, balancing expressive type and structured layouts to create a cohesive editorial experience while preserving each contributor’s individuality.
