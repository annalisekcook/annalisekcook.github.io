HOW I WORK

From first question to final file.

A clear, collaborative process that keeps every project strategic, on-brand and on schedule.

  1. Research

    I start by learning the business, its audience and its competitors, asking the questions that shape a clear creative brief.

  2. Concept

    Mood boards, sketches and rough directions let us explore ideas quickly and agree on a direction before the details.

  3. Design

    I build the chosen direction into a cohesive system: logo, type, color, layouts and every digital touchpoint.

  4. Refine

    Feedback rounds, checks across sizes and devices, and polished final files ready for print, web and social.
